Audio transcription (Gemini, optional)

Claude cannot hear audio. To turn audio into text (Auto mode and Improve with Gemini) you need a Gemini key. Without it, use Instant browser text.

Keys are stored only in this browser and are never in the page code. Because this page is public on GitHub, do not use it on a shared computer. For the Claude key, set a low monthly spend limit in the Anthropic Console. For the Gemini key, restrict it to your github.io address.
Free Gemini plans may let Google use submitted content to improve its products. Check Google's current terms, and Anthropic's, before using real recordings or transcripts of teachers. I have not verified either company's retention rules.
